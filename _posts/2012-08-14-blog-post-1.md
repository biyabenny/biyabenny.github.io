So far, this course has helped me develop a better understanding of how data science can be applied within psychology and cognitive neuroscience. One of the most valuable parts has been learning how to work with EEG data using Python, beginning with preprocessing and identifying noise, and then progressing towards multi-participant analysis.

I have particularly enjoyed the practical nature of the course because the weekly activities have given me the opportunity to apply what I am learning rather than only studying it theoretically. As a Bachelor of Psychology (Cognitive Neuroscience) student, I have found this especially relevant because EEG and data analysis are closely connected to research in brain function and behaviour.

Some parts of the course have also been challenging, especially coding, troubleshooting errors and understanding different preprocessing steps. However, working through these activities has helped me become more confident with technical problem-solving.

Learning about reproducibility, project organisation, version control and GitHub has also changed the way I think about research. I now have a better understanding of the importance of keeping research organised, transparent and reproducible.

As the course continues, I am looking forward to learning more about reproducible reports and developing my Python and data-analysis skills further.
