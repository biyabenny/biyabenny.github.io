Welcome to my academic website.

My name is Biya Benny, and I am currently in my third and final year of the Bachelor of Psychology (Cognitive Neuroscience). Throughout my studies, I have developed a strong interest in understanding the relationship between brain function, behaviour and psychological wellbeing.

My degree has helped me build knowledge in areas such as cognitive neuroscience, research methods, data analysis and evidence-based psychological practice. I have particularly enjoyed developing a deeper understanding of how research can be used to explore behaviour and support better outcomes in clinical and rehabilitation settings.

I am especially interested in rehabilitation psychology and hope to pursue a career in this area after completing my degree. I am drawn to work that supports people adjusting to injury, illness or major life changes, and I would like to continue developing the practical and research skills needed to contribute meaningfully in this field.

This website is a space to document my academic development, reflect on my learning and showcase some of the skills and experiences I have gained throughout my studies.
